Nasi Goreng Kampung

A fast, fragrant fried rice loaded with garlic, chilies, and crunchy anchovies.

Ingredients:

  • Cooked Rice
  • Dried Anchovies
  • Shallots
  • Garlic

Steps:

  1. Fry the anchovies
  2. Mince shallots,garlic and chillies then saute in oil until fragrant
  3. Scramble egg in the pan, toss all the ingredients and stir fry over high heat until well combined
